# Product Requirements

## Product statement

CarePath guides hospital patients through a visit by combining visit/service state with indoor location and routing.

## Primary users

- Patient / caregiver using LINE OA and LIFF
- Hospital staff configuring service points and flow
- IT/integration team connecting CarePath to the HIS

## Primary patient goals

1. Know the current step.
2. Know the next step.
3. Know where the next service is.
4. Receive a simple route to that destination.
5. Avoid having to understand hospital department structure.

## Primary hospital goals

1. Reduce navigation confusion.
2. Reduce repetitive wayfinding questions to staff.
3. Make patient flow visible without replacing the HIS.
4. Allow floor plans and service flows to evolve independently.
5. Support gradual rollout from QR location to richer indoor positioning.
